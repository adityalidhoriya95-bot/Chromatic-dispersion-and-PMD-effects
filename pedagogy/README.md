#### 1. Focus Area

To reinforce the theoretical concepts of chromatic dispersion and polarization mode dispersion (PMD) in optical fiber communication, and understand their effects on pulse broadening and high-speed data transmission.

#### 2. Learning Objectives and Cognitive Level

| Sr. No. | Learning Objective | Cognitive Level | Action Verb |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| 1. | Explain chromatic dispersion and its effect on optical pulse broadening. | Understand | Explain |
| 2. | Analyze the effect of fiber length and spectral width on chromatic dispersion. | Analyze | Analyze |
| 3. | Investigate the effect of PMD on pulse broadening and high-speed optical links. | Analyze | Investigate |

#### 3. Instructional Strategy

###### Name of Instructional Strategy: <u>Guided Simulation-Based Learning</u>

###### Assessment Method: Formative Assessment through Simulation Tasks and Conceptual Questions

<u><b>Description:</b></u>

Students will first review the concepts of chromatic dispersion and polarization mode dispersion in optical fibers. They will then use the virtual simulator to change parameters such as data rate, fiber length, and spectral width, wherever available.

Students will observe the changes in optical pulse shape and pulse broadening, compare the results for different parameter values, and answer assessment questions based on their observations. The activity connects theoretical concepts with practical simulation results.

#### 4. Task & Assessment Questions

| Sr. No. | Learning Objective | Task to be performed by the student in the simulator | Assessment Questions as per LO & Task |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| 1. | Explain chromatic dispersion and pulse broadening. | Run the simulation and observe the input and output optical pulses. | What is chromatic dispersion, and how does it affect an optical pulse? |
| 2. | Analyze the effect of fiber length and spectral width. | Change the fiber length and spectral width, if available, and compare the output pulse shapes. | How do fiber length and spectral width affect chromatic-dispersion-induced pulse broadening? |
| 3. | Investigate PMD in high-speed links. | Select the PMD experiment or parameter, if available, and compare the pulse response for different settings. | What is PMD, and why can it limit high-speed optical communication? |
