### 1. Open the simulation. You will see a laser source (blue), an optical fiber and a receiver (green).
2. Use the **Fiber Length** slider to set the length of the fiber (in km).
3. Use the **Data Rate** slider to set the transmission rate (in Gb/s).
4. Watch the light pulse travel from the laser source to the receiver. Use **Pause Pulse** to stop it and **Reset** to start again.
5. Use the **Zoom In / Zoom Out** and **Rotate Left / Rotate Right** buttons to view the fiber from different angles.
6. Click **Calculate Results** to see the pulse broadening caused by chromatic dispersion and PMD.
7. Change the fiber length and note the results. Then change the data rate and note the results.
8. Click **Enter VR Mode** to view the lab with a VR headset (a compatible headset is required).
9. Record your observations in a table with these columns: fiber length, data rate, chromatic dispersion (ps), PMD (ps).
10. Answer these questions from your observations:
    - How does the pulse broadening change when the fiber length is doubled?
    - Which grows faster with length, chromatic dispersion or PMD?
    - Why does a higher data rate make the link more sensitive to dispersion?
